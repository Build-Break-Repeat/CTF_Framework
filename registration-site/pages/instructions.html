How to Play

Overview

Welcome to the Build Break Repeat Capture the Flag platform. This environment is designed to help users learn and practice cybersecurity skills through hands-on challenges in a affordable and intuitive way.

Step 1: Register Your Team

Navigate to the Register icon at the top of this page and enter your team information and players. This allows for progress to be tracked during the competition.

Step 2: Choose a Challenge

One registered, you will automatically be redirected to the Challenges page. This is a list of challenges to choose from and this is also where you will be turning in your flags.

Step 3: Find the Flag

Analyze the system, identify vulnerabilities, and exploit them to uncover the hidden flag.
